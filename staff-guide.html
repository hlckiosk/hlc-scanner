🔍 Looking Up a Member
1
Click Member Lookup & QR Code to expand the section.
2
Type the member's name or member ID in the search box.
3
Press Enter or click Search.
4
The member's account card appears showing Member 1, Member 2, dependents with ages, and status.
✉️ Emailing a QR Code
1
Look up the member using the steps above.
2
Click ✉ Email QR Code.
3
Wait for the confirmation: ✓ QR code emailed to [Name].
4
Click ✕ Close Member Lookup when done.
The email goes to the primary email on file. The member receives their QR code image, member ID, and a link to update their account.
If no email is on file, an error message will appear. Ask the member to update their account.

🔍 Looking Up a Member's Passes
1
Click Guest Pass Management to expand the section.
2
Type the member's name or member ID and press Enter or click Search.
3
The account card shows name, status, pass balances, and all members on the account.
✅ Using a Pass (Staff Override)

If a member needs to use a pass at the desk rather than at the kiosk:

1
Look up the member.
2
Click Use Pass (green button).
3
Select Single Pass or Family Pass.
4
Enter the number of guests.
5
Enter guest name(s) — separate multiple names with commas.
6
Check the area(s) being used.
7
Click Use Pass & Check In.
The pass balance decrements automatically, the check-in is logged, and the Last Pass Used date is recorded.
📜 Viewing Pass History

Click the History button next to any member to see a full log of every pass transaction — date, time, pass type, action, change amount, and running balance.

The PassHistory tab in the Members spreadsheet has the full transaction log. Columns AR (Single), AS (Family), and AT (Last Used) show the current state.

3
